# antaḥkaraṇa

English candidates: **inner instrument**.

## Root and derivation

**Root checked:** Dhātupāṭha 8.10, `ḍukṛñ karaṇe`: kṛ in doing/making. [Text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html). This checks the root entry, not a complete derivation of the noun. antar + karaṇa is the compound analysis, with internal sandhi; the exact affix rule has not been checked.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 33

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
antaḥkaraṇaṃ trividhaṃ daśadhā bāhyaṃ trayasya viṣayākhyam /
sāmpratakālaṃ bāhyaṃ trikālam ābhyantaraṃ karaṇam //
```

Literal English: The inner instrument is threefold; the external is tenfold and is called the domain of the three. The external [instrument] concerns the present time; the inner instrument concerns the three times.

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 23

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
adhyavasāyo buddhir dharmo jñānaṃ virāga aiśvaryam /
sāttvikam etadrūpaṃ tāmasam asmād viparyastam //
```

Literal English: Buddhi is determination. Virtue, knowledge, dispassion and mastery are its sattvic form; the opposite of this is tamasic.

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 24

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
abhimāno 'haṃkāras tasmād dvividhaḥ pravartate sargaḥ /
ekādaśakaś ca gaṇas tanmātraḥ pañcakaś caiva //
```

Literal English: Ahaṃkāra is self-appropriation; from it a twofold creation proceeds: the group of eleven and the fivefold group of subtle elements.

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 27

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
ubhayātmakam atra manaḥ saṃkalpakam indriyaṃ ca sādharmyāt /
guṇapariṇāmaviśeṣān nānātvam bāhyabhedāc ca //
```

Literal English: Here the mind is of both natures, forming conceptions, and is a faculty through similarity of properties; diversity is from differences in transformations of the guṇas and from external difference.

## Reading of the proposed English

Inner instrument is faithful. The three are understood from the preceding definition of buddhi, ahaṃkāra and manas. Equating these with two machine aspects is an analogy, not the text’s count.
