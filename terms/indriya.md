# indriya

English candidates: **faculty; sense faculty; action faculty**.

## Root and derivation

**Checked Pāṇini 5.2.93:** `indriyam indraliṅgam indradṛṣṭam indrasṛṣṭam indrajuṣṭam indradattam iti vā`. [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/grammatical/data/A.5.2.93.html); [GRETIL text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/6_sastra/1_gram/paninipu.htm). Literal: indriya [as] Indra’s mark, seen by Indra, created by Indra, enjoyed by Indra, or given by Indra. This is a set of alternative traditional analyses, not merely “mark or power”. The sūtra does not say “power” in this list.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 26

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
buddhīndriyāṇi cakṣuḥśrotraghrāṇarasanatvagākhyāni /
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthān karmendriyāny āhuḥ //
```

Literal English: The cognitive faculties are named eye, ear, nose, tongue and skin; speech, hand, foot, anus and generative organ they call the action faculties.

### Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.3.4 (GRETIL KaU 3.4)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_kathopaniSad.htm).

```text
indriyāṇi hayān āhur viṣayāṃs teṣu gocarān /
ātmendriyamanoyuktaṃ bhoktety āhur manīṣiṇaḥ //
```

Literal English: They call the faculties horses, and the objects their ranges; the wise call the one joined with self, faculties and mind the experiencer.

## Reading of the proposed English

Faculty is the safest umbrella for both kinds. Sense is narrower and suits the cognitive five. Caraka’s pañcendriya count is not automatically the Sāṃkhya set of ten.
