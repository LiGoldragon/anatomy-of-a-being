# kapha; śleṣman

English candidates: **kapha; cohesive/lubricating principle**.

## Root and derivation

No root derivation for kapha was given. Its identification with śleṣman in this context and the grammatical derivations need a separate lexical check.

## Textual evidence

### Suśruta Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 15.4.3 (prose)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/susrutasamhita.xml).

```text
sandhisaṃśleṣaṇasnehanaropaṇapūraṇabalasthairyakṛcchleṣmā pañcadhā pravibhakta udakakarmaṇā+anugrahaṃ karoti //
```

Literal English: Śleṣman, divided fivefold, producing joint cohesion, lubrication, healing, filling, strength and stability, assists by the action of water.

### Suśruta Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 21.8

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/susrutasamhita.xml).

```text
visargādānavikṣepaiḥ somasūryānilā yathā /
dhārayanti jagaddehaṃ kaphapittānilāstathā //
```

Literal English: As the moon, sun and wind sustain the body of the world through release, taking up and dispersal, so do kapha, pitta and wind [sustain the body].

## Reading of the proposed English

Cohesion selects a real function but omits lubrication and support. The quoted 15.4.3 calls this śleṣman; 21.8 names kapha in the triad.
