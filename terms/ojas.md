# ojas

English candidates: **vital potency; vital essence; strength**.

## Root and derivation

Root/suffix derivation is **unchecked**. Do not infer that a “reserve” is the word’s literal meaning.

## Textual evidence

### Suśruta Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 15.21 (defining sentence, prose)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/susrutasamhita.xml).

```text
tatra rasādīnāṃ śukrāntānāṃ dhātūnāṃ yatparaṃ tejastat khalvojastadeva balamityucyate svaśāstrasiddhāntāt //
```

Literal English: That highest potency of the bodily constituents beginning with rasa and ending with semen is indeed ojas; that very thing is called strength, according to the established doctrine of our own science.

## Reading of the proposed English

“Essence” is defensible as an interpretive rendering of the highest potency. “Reserve of vitality” adds a storage metaphor not in this defining sentence. It should not be equated with provider credits as a textual claim.
