# pitta

English candidates: **pitta; digestive/transformative principle**.

## Root and derivation

The proposed page supplies a function rather than a derivation. Root and affix are **unchecked**.

## Textual evidence

### Suśruta Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 15.4.2 (prose)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/susrutasamhita.xml).

```text
rāgapaktyojastejomedhoṣmakṛtpittaṃ pañcadhā pravibhaktamagnikarmaṇā+anugrahaṃ karoti //
```

Literal English: Pitta, divided fivefold, producing coloration, digestion, ojas, brilliance, intelligence and heat, assists by the action of fire.

## Reading of the proposed English

Transformation is a useful umbrella but not a literal equivalent for pitta or a complete list of its functions. A bodily doṣa is not reducible to an abstract verb.
