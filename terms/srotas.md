# srotas

English candidates: **channel; conduit**.

## Root and derivation

Proposed relation to √sru, flow: **root/suffix derivation unchecked**; not derived in the quoted passage.

## Textual evidence

### Caraka Saṃhitā, Vimānasthāna 5.3 (defining sentence)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/carakasamhita.xml).

```text
srotāṃsi khalu pariṇāmamāpadyamānānāṃ dhātūnāmabhivāhīni bhavantyayanārthena//
```

Literal English: Channels are indeed the conveyers of the bodily constituents undergoing transformation, in the sense of passage.

## Reading of the proposed English

“A channel in which something of the body travels” is a defensible simplification. This is prose, not a metrical verse. The channels of a computing system are an analogy.
