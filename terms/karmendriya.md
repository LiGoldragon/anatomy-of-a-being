# karmendriya

English candidates: **action faculty; organ of action**.

## Root and derivation

**Root checked:** Dhātupāṭha 8.10, `ḍukṛñ karaṇe`: kṛ in doing/making. [Text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html). This checks the root entry, not a complete derivation of the noun. karman + indriya contracts to karmendriya. The separate formation of karman remains unchecked.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 26

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
buddhīndriyāṇi cakṣuḥśrotraghrāṇarasanatvagākhyāni /
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthān karmendriyāny āhuḥ //
```

Literal English: The cognitive faculties are named eye, ear, nose, tongue and skin; speech, hand, foot, anus and generative organ they call the action faculties.

## Reading of the proposed English

Organ of action is conventional. Speech is an action faculty; the mouth as an anatomical organ is not simply identical to the faculty.
