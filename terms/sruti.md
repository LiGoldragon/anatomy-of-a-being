# śruti

English candidates: **hearing; received sacred revelation**.

## Root and derivation

**Root checked:** Dhātupāṭha 1.1092, `śru śravaṇe`: śru in hearing. [Text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html). This checks the root entry, not a complete derivation of the noun. The full noun derivation remains unchecked.

## Textual evidence

**Unchecked:** no defining primary-text passage has yet been verified for this page.

## Reading of the proposed English

“What was heard” is a conventional explanatory gloss, but śruti is an action/result noun, not itself the past participle śruta. “Hearing” is morphologically closer. No defining verse has yet been checked.
