# sākṣin

English candidates: **witness; eyewitness**.

## Root and derivation

The proposal’s `sa + akṣa` is an incomplete explanatory split. A more specific grammatical source is checked:

```text
sākṣād draṣṭari saṃjñāyām
```

Pāṇini 5.2.91, [The Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/grammatical/data/A.5.2.91.html). Literal: from sākṣāt, in the sense of a seer, as a designation.

The [Kāśikāvṛtti on 5.2.91](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/6_sastra/1_gram/jvkasixu.htm) makes the derivation explicit:

```text
sākṣācchabdo 'vyayam /
tasmād iniḥ pratyayo bhavati draṣṭari vācye /
sākṣād draṣṭā sākṣī /
```

Literal: Sākṣāt is an indeclinable. From it the suffix ini occurs when a seer is to be expressed. A direct seer is sākṣī.

Sākṣin is the stem; sākṣī is its nominative singular. The commentary distinguishes the observer from the giver or receiver in the witnessed transaction. The deeper formation of sākṣāt, and the full account of the final-segment deletion, are not claimed checked here. One Sanskrit Documents rendering has inconsistent navigation numbers (5.2.90 versus 5.2.91); the Sanskrit Library and GRETIL numbering agree on 5.2.91.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 19

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
tasmāc ca viparyāsāt siddhaṃ sākṣitvam asya puruṣasya /
kaivalyaṃ mādhyasthyaṃ draṣṭṛtvam akartṛbhāvaś ca //
```

Literal English: And from that contrast are established this puruṣa’s witnesshood, isolation, neutrality, seerhood and non-agency.

## Reading of the proposed English

Witness is directly supported by sākṣitva in Kārikā 19. The verse’s non-agency is a doctrine about puruṣa, not evidence that an actual living reader never acts.
