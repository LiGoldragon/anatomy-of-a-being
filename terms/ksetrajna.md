# kṣetrajña

English candidates: **field-knower; knower**.

## Root and derivation

kṣetra + jña is a transparent compound analysis. The root √jñā and the exact nominal affix still need a checked grammatical source.

## Textual evidence

### Bhagavad Gītā 13.1 (critical-text numbering)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/2_epic/mbh/ext/bhgce__u.htm).

```text
idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetram ity abhidhīyate /
etad yo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tadvidaḥ //
```

Literal English: This body, son of Kuntī, is called the field. Whoever knows it, those who know that call him the field-knower.

## Reading of the proposed English

Knower is faithful when the field is already understood. The field-knower is distinguished from the field; the verse does not allocate a machine department to either.
