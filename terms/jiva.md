# jīva

English candidates: **living being; living one**.

## Root and derivation

**Root checked:** Dhātupāṭha 1.643, `jīva prāṇadhāraṇe`: jīv in sustaining vital breath/life. [Text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html). This checks the root entry, not a complete derivation of the noun. A complete nominal derivation is unchecked.

## Reading of the proposed English

The living one is a reasonable lexical rendering. No defining occurrence from Caraka, Suśruta or Sāṃkhya Kārikā has yet been checked for this page. This root entry alone does not establish that jīva and ātman are interchangeable in every doctrine.
