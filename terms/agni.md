# agni

English candidates: **fire; digestive fire**.

## Root and derivation

A grammatical or historical derivation of agni is **unchecked**. Its literal lexical sense is fire.

## Textual evidence

### Caraka Saṃhitā, Cikitsāsthāna 15.5

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus/blob/1eac9ee0b055c8d11147edac4a75c76008ccc363/carakasamhita.xml).

```text
yadannaṃ dehadhātvojobalavarṇādipoṣakam/
```

Literal English: For food’s nourishing bodily constituents, ojas, strength, complexion and so forth, fire is the cause; for rasa and the rest do not arise from undigested food.

## Reading of the proposed English

“Fire, which transforms what is taken in” is a good functional account in this context, not an exhaustive definition of all uses of agni. “Burning low or overfed creates residue” needs a more precise separate source.
