# viṣaya

English candidates: **object; domain; range**.

## Root and derivation

Proposed alternatives vi + √si and √viṣ are **disputed/unchecked here**. No checked Dhātupāṭha entry or suffix rule yet decides between them. “What a sense is bound to” must remain an explanatory hypothesis.

## Textual evidence

### Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.3.4 (GRETIL KaU 3.4)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_kathopaniSad.htm).

```text
indriyāṇi hayān āhur viṣayāṃs teṣu gocarān /
ātmendriyamanoyuktaṃ bhoktety āhur manīṣiṇaḥ //
```

Literal English: They call the faculties horses, and the objects their ranges; the wise call the one joined with self, faculties and mind the experiencer.

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 33

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
antaḥkaraṇaṃ trividhaṃ daśadhā bāhyaṃ trayasya viṣayākhyam /
sāmpratakālaṃ bāhyaṃ trikālam ābhyantaraṃ karaṇam //
```

Literal English: The inner instrument is threefold; the external is tenfold and is called the domain of the three. The external [instrument] concerns the present time; the inner instrument concerns the three times.

## Reading of the proposed English

Range and object are good contextual renderings. Gocara is pasture/range in the chariot verse, not literally a constructed road. The proposal’s roads are a readable analogy.
