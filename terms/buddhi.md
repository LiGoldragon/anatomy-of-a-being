# buddhi

English candidates: **intellect; determination; discernment**.

## Root and derivation

**Root checked:** Dhātupāṭha 4.68, `budha avagamane`: budh in understanding. [Text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html). This checks the root entry, not a complete derivation of the noun. “Awakeness” is a suggestive root gloss, not the technical definition. The full noun formation is unchecked.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 23

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
adhyavasāyo buddhir dharmo jñānaṃ virāga aiśvaryam /
sāttvikam etadrūpaṃ tāmasam asmād viparyastam //
```

Literal English: Buddhi is determination. Virtue, knowledge, dispassion and mastery are its sattvic form; the opposite of this is tamasic.

### Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.3.3 (GRETIL KaU 3.3)

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_kathopaniSad.htm).

```text
ātmānaṃ rathinaṃ viddhi śarīraṃ ratham eva tu /
buddhiṃ tu sārathiṃ viddhi manaḥ pragraham eva ca //
```

Literal English: Know the self as the chariot-owner, and the body as the chariot itself; know buddhi as the charioteer and the mind as the rein.

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 20

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
tasmāt tatsaṃyogād acetanaṃ cetanāvad iva liṅgam /
guṇakartṛtve ca tathā karteva bhavaty udāsīnaḥ //
```

Literal English: Therefore, from conjunction with that, the unconscious subtle body appears as if conscious; likewise, though the guṇas act, the indifferent one appears as if an agent.

## Reading of the proposed English

The proposal correctly cites determination (adhyavasāya). Discernment is a reasonable functional name; “the Awake” is too loose. Kārikā 20 says “as if conscious”; the stronger claim of a reflected copy of the self is not stated there and needs a named commentary.

## Further grammatical check

**Checked general suffix rule:** Pāṇini 3.3.94, `striyāṃ ktin`, [Sanskrit Library](https://www.sanskritlibrary.org/grammatical/data/A.3.3.94.html), supplies ktin in the feminine. This supports analysis as a feminine action/state noun from budh, not the English adjective “awake”. A full derivation must also account for the consonant changes; that proof is not claimed here.
