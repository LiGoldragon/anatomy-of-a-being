# jñānendriya

English candidates: **cognitive faculty; sense faculty**.

## Root and derivation

jñāna + indriya is the compound analysis; its complete derivation is **unchecked**.

## Textual evidence

### Sāṃkhya Kārikā 26

Status: **checked against the Sanskrit e-text**, not collated against a manuscript or printed scan. [Text](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_IzvarakRSNa-sAMkhyakArikA.htm).

```text
buddhīndriyāṇi cakṣuḥśrotraghrāṇarasanatvagākhyāni /
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthān karmendriyāny āhuḥ //
```

Literal English: The cognitive faculties are named eye, ear, nose, tongue and skin; speech, hand, foot, anus and generative organ they call the action faculties.

## Reading of the proposed English

The five cognitive faculties are correct, but the checked Kārikā’s own word is buddhīndriya, not jñānendriya. Keep the synonymous later label distinct from the word actually quoted.

## Further grammatical check

**Checked root:** Dhātupāṭha 9.43, `jñā avabodhane`, in understanding ([text](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_major_works/dhatupatha.html)). This confirms the knowing root, not every step of the compound’s derivation.
