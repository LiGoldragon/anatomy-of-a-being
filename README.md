# Anatomy of a being

A source-checked study of the anatomy of a being in the Sanskrit śāstras: one page per term, Sanskrit passages in IAST, literal English, documented derivations and English candidates. The purpose is to understand the texts before borrowing their terms for a machine.

- [Index by term](indexes/terms.md)
- [Index by text](indexes/texts.md)
- [Verification method and sources](SOURCES.md)
- [Verse and doctrinal checks](VERSE-CHECKS.md)

This working collection checks the medical fourfold definition of life, the tripod, the five vital winds, channels, fire and ojas, the chariot and field verses, and the Sāṃkhya inner instrument. It distinguishes a checked quotation from an unchecked derivation and from a modern design analogy. It is a working research edition, not medical advice or a critical edition.
