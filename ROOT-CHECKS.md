# Root and derivation audit

The two proposal tables often give a root and then translate the noun by the root’s image. This audit distinguishes three things: the root entry, the noun formation, and the word’s contextual meaning. “Unchecked” means no source proof is claimed, even where an analysis is linguistically plausible.

## Comparative claims outside Sanskrit grammar

The assertions that Greek psychē derives from blowing/breathing, that ātman is historically breath, and that manas, mens and mind share inherited material require historical-linguistic sources. They have **not been verified by this Sanskrit grammar audit**. A similarity of image does not establish synonymy, doctrinal identity or an inherited word shared between languages.
