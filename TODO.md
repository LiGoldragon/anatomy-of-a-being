# Remaining verification

All terms in the two initial tables now have pages, and all explicitly quoted verses have been checked in the cited e-texts. The work is not a completed philological edition.

- Complete noun derivations remain unchecked wherever each term page says so. A checked root entry is not a checked derivation.
- jīva, citta, śruti, deha and kāya still need defining passages in their relevant primary texts. A missing defining quotation is recorded, not replaced from memory.
- viṣaya and sākṣin require resolved grammatical derivations; ātman’s historical etymology and the Greek/Indo-European claims need historical-linguistic evidence.
- āma still needs an explicit defining passage in the requested source set.
- The reflection claim needs a checked commentary; the root Kārikā alone does not establish it.
- Collation with the local Sanskrit scanned editions and their variants has not been performed.
