# Verse and doctrinal checks

| Proposed reference | Check and correction | Term pages |
|---|---|---|
| Caraka Sūtrasthāna 1.42 | The half-verse is accurate; the full verse adds synonyms of life. | [āyus](terms/ayus.md) |
| Caraka Sūtrasthāna 1.46–47 | 1.46 is accurate. 1.47 calls the composite pumān and conscious, the subject of the science. Neither verse explicitly says the senses are folded into the body. | [ātman](terms/atman.md), [puruṣa](terms/purusa.md) |
| Kaṭha 1.3.3–4 | The proposal quotes all of 3 and only the first half of 4, correctly. Full 4 also names the composite experiencer. GRETIL numbers the six sections continuously: KaU 3.3–4. “Roads” is looser than ranges/pastures. | [manas](terms/manas.md), [viṣaya](terms/visaya.md) |
| Gītā 13.1 | Accurate in the critical-text convention used here; 13.2 when Arjuna’s opening question is counted. | [kṣetra](terms/ksetra.md) |
| Gītā’s field list | 13.5–6 here, 13.6–7 with the extra opening question. Includes mental functions and unmanifest nature, not only the physical body. | [kṣetra](terms/ksetra.md) |
| Sāṃkhya buddhi as adhyavasāya | Explicit in Kārikā 23. | [buddhi](terms/buddhi.md) |
| Sāṃkhya inner instrument | Threefold in Kārikā 33; the prior definitions of 23, 24 and 27 identify the three. | [antaḥkaraṇa](terms/antahkarana.md) |
| Self reflected in intellect | Not stated in that form in the checked root verses. Kārikā 20 says the unconscious liṅga is as if conscious through conjunction. A reflection doctrine requires a named commentary and checked passage. | [buddhi](terms/buddhi.md) |

The chariot, tripod and field analyses are different textual models, not one interchangeable doctrine. The proposed machine correspondences remain design proposals.
