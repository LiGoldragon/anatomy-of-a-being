## Five winds: Caraka and Suśruta compared

Caraka Cikitsāsthāna 28.5 names all five and says that, moving unobstructed in their seats, they govern the body. Verses 6–11 give their seats and actions. They broadly agree with Suśruta Nidānasthāna 1.13–19, while selecting different details; the five English labels are functional selections, rather than exhaustive definitions.

| Wind | Caraka’s emphasis | Consequence for the proposed English |
|---|---|---|
| [prāṇa](terms/prana.md) | Several upper-body seats; breathing, food and expulsive actions such as sneezing. | Intake selects only part of the account. |
| [udāna](terms/udana.md) | Navel, chest and throat; speech, effort, energy, strength and complexion. | Expression captures speech, with a wider bodily range left over. |
| [samāna](terms/samana.md) | Channels and support of the internal digestive fire. | Digestive coordination fits both accounts; gathering information is a modern analogy. |
| [vyāna](terms/vyana.md) | Pervasion of the whole body and movements, including blinking. | Distribution fits its extent, with motor activity also central. |
| [apāna](terms/apana.md) | Lower-body seats; urinary, faecal and reproductive discharge. | Release includes reproduction and childbirth. |
